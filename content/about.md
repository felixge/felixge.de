I'm a software engineer living in Berlin, Germany with my partner and two kids.

Since 2021, I've been working on Datadog's [Continuous Profiler](https://www.datadoghq.com/product/code-profiling/) product with a focus on Go profiling. I also work on OpenTelemetry, internal cost efficiency and various other things.

Before that, I spent 6.5 years building a factory traceability system for Apple. Your Apple device probably has a barcode from it.

Prior to that, I co-founded and bootstrapped [Transloadit](http://transloadit.com/) into a profitable business.

I've also created a [lot of open source](https://github.com/felixge) over the years. Most notable are my contributions to the Go runtime ([50+ patches](https://go-review.googlesource.com/q/owner:felix.geisendoerfer@datadoghq.com+status:merged), e.g. [frame pointer unwinding](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0f8JnJhC0-0&t=0s)) and being one of the first Node.js core contributors ([100+ patches](https://github.com/nodejs/node/commits?author=felixge), also created [node-mysql](https://github.com/mysqljs/mysql) and [node-formidable](https://github.com/node-formidable/formidable)).

Back when drones were fun and harmless, [a few friends](https://web.archive.org/web/20240424231729/http://www.nodecopter.com/core) and I organized [NodeCopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20250105164856/https://www.nodecopter.com/), a hackathon for programming flying robots with JavaScript. It inspired about 30 satellite events and some [academic literature](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?hl=en&as_sdt=0%2C5&q=node-ar-drone&btnG=).

Last but not least, I enjoy playing competitive beach volleyball on a weekly basis.
